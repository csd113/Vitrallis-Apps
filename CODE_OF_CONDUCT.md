# Code of Conduct

Vitrallis welcomes people with different backgrounds, devices, and levels of
experience. Help make issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces
useful places to learn and build.

## Working together

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people new to Linux, small-board
  hardware, Python, or Git. Explain assumptions and unfamiliar terms.
- Critique the work with concrete examples. Disagree without personal attacks,
  and accept corrections when new evidence changes the picture.
- Respect contributors' time: keep reports focused, avoid repeated demands, and
  do not pressure people to test on hardware or share private information.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, use sexualized language or imagery,
  publish another person's private information, or spam project spaces.

These expectations apply in project spaces and when representing Vitrallis.
Maintainers may edit or remove disruptive content, close or lock discussions,
warn participants, or restrict participation according to the conduct and its
impact. Apply these expectations consistently, including to maintainers.

## Raising a concern

For public conduct concerns, contact a maintainer in the affected GitHub
conversation when doing so is safe. Avoid repeating harmful content. If the
concern needs privacy, request a private contact method from the repository
owner via a method on their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/csd113); no
project-specific private moderation address is currently published. Do not post
personal details while requesting a channel. GitHub's own reporting controls are
also available for abuse on the platform, including concerns involving maintainers.

Report technical vulnerabilities through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), and ordinary
usage problems through [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md).
